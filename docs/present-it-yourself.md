# Present it yourself

**You get** the video as slides, to give in your own voice.

## Steps

1. **Make the video first** (see [Your first video](first-video.md)).
2. **Ask for "a deck to present it myself".** You get, beside the video:

   | File | What it is |
   |---|---|
   | `<name>.pptx` | a slide per sentence, the sentence as its speaker note, a section per chapter |
   | `<name>.script.md` | the narration to read aloud, each sentence with its slide and its time in the video |
   | `<name>.srt` | the narration as subtitles, for a video editor |
   | `<name>.silent.mp4` | the video without its sound |

3. **Open the deck** in PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides or LibreOffice, and present.

## Slides that move

Ask for slides that move: each slide then plays its part of the animation as it opens, from the previous slide's
moment to its own. PowerPoint and Keynote play it; Google Slides and other apps show a still. The deck is then about
the size of the video.

## The click-through version

Ask for the click-through version: the animated video in your browser, a step per sentence. A click or → plays the
next step and waits, ← goes back, and S opens the speaker notes in a second window. Claude starts it and gives you
the address; it runs until stopped.

A slide and a step show the same moment: just after the sentence has been said, before the scene's content leaves.
